Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions — you might find what you’re looking for here.

1How long does it take to make my order?
Most orders are handmade within 3-5 business days. During busy periods, this may extend to 7-10 days. We’ll always let you know the expected timescale when you order.
2Can I request a custom scent or design?
Absolutely! We love creating custom orders. Tell us what you’re looking for and we’ll work with you to create something perfect.
3Do you offer gift wrapping?
Yes! Our products come beautifully packaged and gift-ready. Special messages can be included with your order.
4What if I’m not happy with my order?
Your satisfaction is our priority. Please contact us within 7 days if there is an issue and we’ll make it right.
5Do you deliver nationwide?
Yes, we deliver throughout the UK. Delivery times and costs vary by location and are shown at checkout.
6Can I place a bulk order for an event?
Yes! Contact us with your event details, quantity, and timeline and we’ll create a custom quote.
