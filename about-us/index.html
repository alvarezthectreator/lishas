About Lishas Handmade Creations

Every creation tells a story. Welcome to our world of handmade treasures, made one piece at a time in our UK studio.

From a small idea to a thriving handmade business

Our Story

“What started as a hobby creating gifts for family and friends has blossomed into a passion for bringing joy to homes across the UK.”

Lishas Handmade Creations was born from a love of crafting beautiful, personalised items that bring happiness to everyday life. Every product we create is made with the same care and attention we’d give to gifts for our own loved ones.

From our UK studio, we handcraft everything from luxurious bath products to gorgeous home fragrances, each one made to order with your personal touch. Whether it’s choosing your favourite scent, adding a special name, or selecting the perfect colour, we believe that personalisation transforms a product into a treasured keepsake.

“Our mission is simple: to create beautiful, handmade products that bring a smile to your face and make your everyday moments feel special. Every item that leaves our studio carries a piece of the love and care we put into making it.”

Today, we’re proud to offer over 20 different product ranges — from wax melts and bath bombs to personalised tumblers and planners. Each one is still handmade with the same passion and dedication that started it all.

The values that guide everything we create

What We Believe In

Made with Love

Every single item is handcrafted with genuine care and passion. We pour our heart into everything we make because we know it’s going to someone special.
